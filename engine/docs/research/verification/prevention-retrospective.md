## Method

Every available formal finding file and every current architecture finding ID is
discovered from supplied facts rather than a handwritten count. Three delegated
read-only replay reports, the Ajv strict-type reproduction, the bounded static
authority scan, the current edit-tool boundary, and explicit unavailable or
tainted-source dispositions extend that corpus. The reproduced MWA receipt-
builder default-effect gap reuses `FOUNDATION-004`; it is not a new finding.
Each incident must name:

- exact sources and their current availability;
- one or more of `centrally-prevented-narrowly`, `prevention-missing`,
  `capability-unavailable`, and `exact-history-unrecoverable`;
- a centralized owner and reuse targets;
- a pre-effect, pre-claim, or combined enforcement boundary;
- a hostile regression with at least three repeated runs and three materially
  distinct scenarios;
- applicable modules, languages, and surfaces;
- remaining closure evidence, claim limits, and a concrete research and
  qualification path; and
- an explicit statement that model vigilance is defense in depth only.

The pure verifier rejects omitted or duplicate sources and incidents, unknown
rows, false central-prevention classifications, empty prevention scope, empty
research paths, model-vigilance-only dispositions, self-approval, and dishonest
unavailable or confidentiality-tainted source dispositions. The test suite runs
three distinct hostile mutation families three times each.
